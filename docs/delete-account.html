Delete Your Account — Chess

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Chess account and all associated data, whether or not you still have the app installed.

If you have the app installed

Open Chess, sign in, and on the home screen tap Delete account. Confirming immediately and permanently deletes your account and your online games.

If you don't have the app installed

Email luke-gplay-developer@googlegroups.com from the email address associated with your account (the Google account you signed in with) with the subject line "Delete my Chess account". We will process your request within 60 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name and email address
  • Every online game you are a player in, including its moves, for both players

Deleting your account also deletes your sign-in identity itself, so you would need to sign in again as a new account to play online afterward. Games saved only on your device (against the computer, on a shared device, or imported) are not stored by us; uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove them.

What we retain

Nothing, beyond what we may be legally required to retain. We don't currently have any such requirement.

Contact

Questions about account deletion: luke-gplay-developer@googlegroups.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
